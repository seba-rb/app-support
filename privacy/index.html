kttl

kttl controls a compatible kettle from an iPhone. No account is required. Favorites, observed sessions, preferences, and kettle pairing information are stored on the iPhone. Notifications are generated on the device. kttl does not operate a server that receives kettle data.

During setup, kttl sends the Wi‑Fi network name and password to the kettle using encrypted Bluetooth. If you leave Remember network and password enabled, kttl stores a copy in the iPhone Keychain without syncing it to other devices. kttl exchanges identity, state, readings, and commands with the kettle over Bluetooth or the local network. When kttl uses the LAN interface, HTTP communication stays on the local network. The Wi‑Fi password is sent through the Bluetooth setup channel.

You can delete favorites in the app and remove the pairing and stored credentials using the forget options. The kettle retains its own network configuration. You can revoke Bluetooth, Local Network, and Notifications access in iOS Settings. The app limits local history and diagnostic storage. Backups depend on your iOS settings.
